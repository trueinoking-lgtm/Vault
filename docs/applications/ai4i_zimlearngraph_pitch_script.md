# Pitch Script: ZimLearnGraph

> **AI4I Data Track — Oral Presentation Script (~3 minutes)**

**Track:** Data Track

---

## Opening (30 seconds)

> "Zimbabwean schools already generate learning data every week. The problem is that the data is trapped in mark books, spreadsheets, and term reports, so it cannot guide timely intervention or power responsible AI systems.
>
> ZimLearnGraph is an AI-ready education data layer that converts those ordinary teacher-marked assessments into structured, privacy-conscious learning evidence. The dataset is the innovation. The dashboard is proof. AI is advisory."

---

## The Problem (30 seconds)

> "Every term, Zimbabwean teachers mark thousands of tests. Each marked test contains granular signals — which topics a learner struggled with, which questions exposed misconceptions, which learners need support. After the score is recorded, most of that intelligence is lost.
>
> Teachers make intervention decisions from memory. School leaders allocate resources blind. Researchers have no longitudinal dataset. And AI developers cannot build education AI because the foundational training data does not exist in structured form.
>
> This is a data infrastructure problem, not a technology gap."

---

## What ZimLearnGraph Does (45 seconds)

> "ZimLearnGraph does not ask schools to change how they teach or assess. It asks them to capture what they already know in structured form.
>
> A school registers its classes and teachers. We generate anonymous learner codes — no names ever enter the system. The teacher enters assessment metadata and maps each question to a curriculum topic from the ZIMSEC syllabus. They record marks via web form or offline CSV upload.
>
> Our analytics engine derives topic-level performance per learner and flags weak topics automatically. When a teacher intervenes — reteaching, extra exercises — they log it. When they administer a follow-up assessment, the system compares pre and post scores and shows whether the intervention worked.
>
> This is the complete data chain: mark entry → topic performance → weakness signal → intervention → follow-up result."

---

## Why Data Track (30 seconds)

> "We are applying to the Data Track because the dataset is the core innovation.
>
> The dashboard is a proof layer showing what the data enables. But a dashboard without data is an empty interface. The data pipeline without a dashboard is still a functioning, impact-generating dataset.
>
> The hard work is creating clean, structured, topic-linked, privacy-conscious education data where none existed in usable form. The hard work is the data layer."

---

## What Already Exists (30 seconds)

> "We are not starting from zero.
>
> A complete MVP exists today with seeded data demonstrating the full assessment-to-intervention flow. Seven published documents specify every layer of the pipeline — dataset card, data dictionary, collection protocol, quality framework, privacy governance, schema, strategy. A public landing page is live. The analytics engine is built.
>
> We have moved from concept to working proof. We are ready for the pilot that turns this into a real, validated dataset."

---

## AI Is Advisory (20 seconds)

> "ZimLearnGraph treats AI as an advisory layer, not a decision layer.
>
> Topic performance, weakness signals, and intervention effectiveness are computed deterministically — by defined formulas, not AI. AI generates natural-language summaries of what the analytics have already computed. Every AI output is labelled: advisory only, teacher-check-required.
>
> There is no automated pass/fail. No automated labelling. No automated decisions affecting learners."

---

## What a Pilot Would Prove (20 seconds)

> "A pilot across 5 to 10 schools would validate that the collection protocol works in real classrooms, that teachers can adopt structured data entry, and that the full data chain operates end-to-end with real data.
>
> The output is a quality-scored, documented, AI-ready dataset — plus the evidence base for wider adoption."

---

## Close (15 seconds)

> "ZimLearnGraph is built. The documents exist. The privacy architecture is designed. What we need is the opportunity to validate with real school data.
>
> The dataset layer is the core innovation. The dashboard is proof. AI is advisory. Impact is measurable."

---
