# Impact Intelligence — Teacher Walkthrough

A step-by-step guide for teachers using Impact Intelligence for the first time.

---

## Before You Start

You will need:
- Your school name
- Your class name and grade
- Your subject name
- Learner codes or names
- Your topics list
- Your assessment questions with max marks
- Your learners' marks

**Estimated time:** 15-20 minutes for initial setup, 5-10 minutes per assessment.

---

## Step 1: Set Up Your School

1. Go to the Impact Intelligence page
2. Click **"Set up school"**
3. Enter:
   - School name
   - District (optional)
   - Province (optional)
   - School type (primary/secondary)
4. Click **Save**

---

## Step 2: Create a Class

1. Click **"Add class"**
2. Select your school
3. Enter:
   - Class name (e.g., Form 1A)
   - Grade level (e.g., 1)
   - Academic year (e.g., 2026)
   - Teacher name (optional)
4. Click **Save**

---

## Step 3: Add Learners

1. Stay on the Class page
2. Click **"Add Learners"** (if available) or navigate to Add learners
3. Enter learner codes one at a time:
   - L001, L002, L003, ...
   - Display name is optional
4. Repeat for all learners in your class

**Tip:** You can enter codes L001 through L030 for a typical class of 30 learners.

---

## Step 4: Create Subject and Topics

1. Click **"Create assessment"** from the home page
2. First, add your subject:
   - Subject name (e.g., Mathematics)
   - Level (e.g., O-Level)
   - Curriculum (e.g., ZIMSEC)
3. Add topics under your subject:
   - Topic name (e.g., Fractions)
   - Strand (optional, e.g., Number)
   - Syllabus code (optional, e.g., MATH-001)
4. Repeat for all topics

---

## Step 5: Create an Assessment

1. Click **"Create assessment"**
2. Fill in:
   - Title (e.g., Term 1 Diagnostic Test)
   - Assessment type (Test/Exam/Quiz/Assignment)
   - Term (e.g., Term 1)
   - Date written
   - Total marks (e.g., 100)
   - Pass mark (e.g., 50)
3. Select your school, class, and subject
4. Click **Save**

---

## Step 6: Map Questions to Topics

1. Your assessment is now listed. Click on it to open.
2. Go to the **Questions** tab
3. Click **"Add question"**
4. For each question:
   - Question number (1, 2, 3, ...)
   - Label (optional, e.g., "Multiple Choice")
   - Max marks (e.g., 10)
   - Topic (select from your topics)
   - Skill type (Knowledge/Comprehension/Application/Analysis)
   - Difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard)
5. Repeat for all questions

**Note:** The progress bar shows how many marks you've assigned out of the total.

---

## Step 7: Enter Marks

1. Go to the **Marks** tab
2. You'll see a grid: rows = learners, columns = questions
3. Click on each cell and enter the learner's score
4. Use keyboard arrows to navigate between cells
5. The grid validates each score (must not exceed the question's max marks)
6. Click **Save marks** when finished

**Tip:** You can see each learner's running total and the overall mark completion percentage.

---

## Step 8: View Results

1. Go to the **Results** tab
2. Review:
   - **Class average** — Overall class performance
   - **Pass rate** — Percentage of learners who passed
   - **Weak topics** — Topics needing revision
   - **Question performance** — How each question was answered
   - **At-risk learners** — Learners who need support

**Important:** All numbers shown are calculated from the marks you entered. They are your source of truth.

---

## Step 9: Review Interventions

1. Go to the **Interventions** tab
2. Review recommended actions:
   - **Critical** — Topics needing immediate re-teaching
   - **High** — Topics needing revision
   - **Medium/Low** — Topics to monitor

Each intervention includes a suggested action you can take.

---

## Step 10: Export and Report

1. **Export CSV:** Click "Export Marks" or "Export Analytics" to download a CSV file
2. **View Report:** Click "View Report" to see a print-friendly version
3. **Print to PDF:** Use your browser's Print function (Ctrl+P) and select "Save as PDF"

---

## Step 11: Generate AI Summary (Optional)

1. Go to the **Results** tab and scroll to **AI-Generated Insights**
2. Click **"Generate Teacher Summary"** for a narrative overview
3. Click **"Generate Intervention Plan"** for a structured plan
4. Click **"Generate Remedial Lesson"** for a lesson outline

**Note:** AI summaries are advisory only. Always use the numbers above as the source of truth.
